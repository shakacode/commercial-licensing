## Plain-language summary (informative, not part of the terms)

ShakaPerf is source-available and trust-based: no license keys, no
activation, nothing phones home. Reading, studying, evaluating for 45
days, education, personal projects, open-source work, and attributed
public reports are free for everyone. ShakaPerf is a development tool: it
never serves your users, so for ShakaPerf the paid question is not
"production or staging" but "ongoing work for an organization". Running
it on private code after the evaluation, in CI, in pull-request checks, in
coding-agent workflows, as a regression gate, or by hand every day is all
the same kind of use, and it is free if you can answer yes to all three
questions: *Do fewer than 10 people work for your organization (counting
all affiliates)? Was its revenue in the last twelve months under US $1M?
Has it raised under US $1M in outside capital, ever?* Charities,
educational institutions, and hospitals are free at any size. If any
answer is no and you are none of those, that use needs ShakaStack Pro: one
subscription per organization that covers ShakaPerf, React on Rails Pro,
and Inertia Rails SSR Cache for every affiliate, repository, pipeline, and
developer, with no limit on organization size. The free environments of
React on Rails Pro (development, CI, staging) are a rule about React on
Rails Pro; they do not make ShakaPerf free. Registration is free and
optional (Section A8). Once a version ships under these terms, those terms
govern that version permanently.
