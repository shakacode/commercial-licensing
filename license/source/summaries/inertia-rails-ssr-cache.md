## Plain-language summary (informative, not part of the terms)

Inertia Rails SSR Cache is trust-based: there is no license key, nothing
phones home, and nothing in the software changes how your app runs based
on license status. Reading the source, development, test, CI, staging,
review apps, education, personal projects, and open-source work are free
for everyone, at any organization size. Production use, including serving
your users or doing background work that supports a live application, is
also free if you can answer yes to all three questions: *Do
fewer than 10 people work for your organization (counting all
affiliates)? Was its revenue in the last twelve months under US $1M? Has
it raised under US $1M in outside capital, ever?* Charities, educational
institutions, and hospitals are free at any size. If any answer is no and
you are none of those, production use needs ShakaStack Pro: one
subscription per organization that covers Inertia Rails SSR Cache, React
on Rails Pro, and ShakaPerf for every affiliate, application, environment,
and developer, with no limit on organization size. The environment
permission applies only to development, testing, verification, or
demonstration before production; cache warming for a live application is
production even in a separate worker or CI environment. The free
environments listed here are a rule about Inertia Rails SSR Cache; each
ShakaStack product's own license says what is free for it. See
https://pro.reactonrails.com/. Once a version ships under these terms,
those terms govern that version permanently.
