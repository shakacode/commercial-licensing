# Part B. Product Schedule: React on Rails Pro

## B1. Software

The Software is React on Rails Pro: the `react_on_rails_pro` Ruby gem, the
`react-on-rails-pro`, `react-on-rails-pro-node-renderer`, and
`react-on-rails-rsc` npm packages, and any other component ShakaCode
distributes under this text as part of React on Rails Pro. The open-source
React on Rails core that the Software builds on is licensed separately
under the MIT License. Dependencies and identified third-party portions keep
their own licenses; in particular, the Meta portions of the RSC webpack plugin
remain under their accompanying MIT notice.

## B2. Use model and Covered Use

React on Rails Pro is served software: it runs inside your application to
render and serve pages.

**Covered Use** means production use: running the Software to serve an
application, website, or service to its end users, whether those users are
the public, your customers, or your own staff, including the rendering,
streaming, and background work that supports it. It includes,
without limitation, internal applications used only by your staff and
applications you operate for your customers. An environment used to serve
real end users, or to do background work that supports a live application,
is production whatever its name and whether or not it receives user requests
directly. Environments used solely to develop, test, verify, or demonstrate
an application before production are not Covered Use of this Product
(Section B3(a)).

This environment rule is a rule about React on Rails Pro only. It does not
make any use of another ShakaStack product free (Section A16).

## B3. Additional free uses

a. development, test, continuous integration, staging, preview, review-app,
   and other environments used solely to develop, verify, or demonstrate an
   application before production, without time limit. This does not cover
   serving real end users or background work that supports a live
   application, even in a separate worker or CI environment.

## B4. Evaluation

During the Evaluation Period you may evaluate the Software in any Covered Use
defined in Section B2, including background work that supports a live
application, solely to decide whether to adopt it. Uses that are free under
Section A4 or Section B3 need no evaluation period.

## B5. Grant limits and Generated Attribution

You may include the Software in the builds and deployments of your
organization's own applications. You may not distribute the Software or
your modifications as a standalone product or library, or as part of a
product whose purpose is to provide the Software's functionality to others
(Section A7).

**"Generated Attribution"** means the HTML comment the Software emits once
in each document it renders, in the form
`<!-- Powered by React on Rails Pro (c) ShakaCode | STATUS -->`, where
STATUS is generated by the Software from the license status.
**"License key"** means the optional credential ShakaCode issues that
identifies your organization in the Software's license-status logs and in
the Generated Attribution. A license key is identity, not enforcement.

Every HTML document rendered with the Software must retain exactly one
complete, unmodified Generated Attribution in its source. You may not
remove, alter, obscure, or suppress it, in free and paid use alike. With a
valid license key the status reads `Licensed`; without a key it reads
`UNLICENSED`; an expired or malformed key reads `LICENSE EXPIRED` or
`INVALID LICENSE`. An `UNLICENSED` status is not by itself a violation,
because free uses need no key. The Software never blocks, degrades, or
reports your use based on license status; it only labels and logs it. Keep
your license key private: it identifies your organization. A related
component that does not itself generate or insert the Generated
Attribution, such as `react-on-rails-rsc`, has no obligation to insert a
second one.
