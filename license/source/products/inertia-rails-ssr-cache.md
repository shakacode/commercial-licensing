# Part B. Product Schedule: Inertia Rails SSR Cache

## B1. Software

The Software is Inertia Rails SSR Cache: the `inertia_rails_ssr_cache` Ruby
gem, the `inertia-rails-ssr-cache` npm package, and any other component
ShakaCode distributes under this text as part of Inertia Rails SSR Cache.
Inertia Rails, the Inertia.js adapters, and other dependencies that the
Software works with are licensed separately by their own authors.

## B2. Use model and Covered Use

Inertia Rails SSR Cache is served software: it runs inside your
application to warm and guard the server-side rendering cache that serves
pages.

**Covered Use** means production use: running the Software to serve an
application, website, or service to its end users, whether those users are
the public, your customers, or your own staff, including the background
work that supports it, such as warming and guarding its server-side
rendering cache. It includes,
without limitation, internal applications used only by your staff and
applications you operate for your customers. An environment used to serve
real end users, or to do background work that supports a live application,
is production whatever its name and whether or not it receives user requests
directly. Environments used solely to develop, test, verify, or demonstrate
an application before production are not Covered Use of this Product
(Section B3(a)).

This environment rule is a rule about Inertia Rails SSR Cache only. It
does not make any use of another ShakaStack product free (Section A16).
